6 tips for better prompts ✨

Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet. A good prompt is the difference between a useless answer and a genuinely helpful one.

🎯

Be specific

Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet — tell the AI exactly what you want.

🎭

Give a role

"Act as a study tutor" changes the tone and depth of the reply.

📐

Set the format

Ask for bullet points, tables, or a 150-word summary — you're in control.

🧩

Give examples

Show the AI one good example of what you want and it will follow your style.

🔄

Iterate, don't restart

Refine your request in the same chat — the AI remembers the context.

🔍

Verify the output

AI can invent facts. Always cross-check anything that matters.
